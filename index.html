Built for the hillside

Steep sites are where we do our best work, from canyon estates to cliffside resorts. Every hillside plan solves three things together: holding the slope, moving the water, and keeping fire away from the buildings.

Zone 0, 0–1.5 m

Gravel, stone and low, irrigated plants next to the house. Nothing that burns easily touches the walls.

Zone 1, 1.5–9 m

Spaced, well-watered planting and terraces you can walk, sit and entertain on.

Zone 2, 9–30 m

Thinned native slope planting with deep roots that hold soil after rain.

Water

Swales and bioretention move storm water away from footings and back into the ground.
